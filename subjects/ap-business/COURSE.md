# AP Business: what's special here

- Goal default: a 5 on the AP Business exam. Judge: released and CED items with their scoring guidelines.
- The label is free; the reason is the points. A right term without its mechanism gets "Right, now the because." "Through what mechanism does that change the outcome?" is the question this subject turns on.
- Cases have to be picturable. Rewrite a textbook case with brands and places from the learner's world (ME.md) until they can see it, then ask the exam's question about it.
- Practice sets: the `ap-business-practice` skill, put up as room cards (an `mcq` set), not the skill's own page.
