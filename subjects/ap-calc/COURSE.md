# AP Calculus AB: what's special here

- Goal default: a 5 on the AP Calculus AB exam. Judge: College Board released FRQs and their scoring guidelines.
- **The learner computes.** Setup and reasoning are theirs, and so is the arithmetic: the exam scores the final value. Check their numbers afterwards in one line.
- Use the exam's variables (θ, t, s), not x. No equation sheet during practice; the exam has none.
- Every justification has the shape conclusion + "because" + the numbers, and names the theorem with its conditions (IVT, MVT, EVT, Rolle). "What licenses this step?" is the question this subject turns on.
- Representations rotate: a skill shown with an equation is not yet shown with a graph, a table or words. Note the forms in each SKILLS.md row's Note (eq, graph, table, words), and change the form on every cold question.
- **Math in chat:** `$…$` renders only when what's inside has a letter or a backslash. Write intervals and bare numbers in plain text ([0, 4], 0) or inside an expression ($x \in [0, 4]$). In the room, `$math$` always renders.
- **Worksheet maps.** Coverage comes from the teacher's worksheets, not the guided notes. When class reaches a chapter, build `practice/worksheet-map-<chapter>.md` from the chapter's worksheets (every problem type they set, and a Tested column). Before a test, every type on the map gets asked once, with new numbers or a new form. Then go past them: the question asked backwards, the same idea as a table, a graph, words and an equation, "must there be an inflection point?".
- **Homework photos.** When the learner sends a photo or PDF of written work, copy it to `uploads/<date>/`, check every exercise (solve each yourself first), reply with the count right and the misses by number, then put the misses up as cards so they fix the wrong step themselves.
- **Mock tests and progress checks:** use the `ap-calc-test` skill.
