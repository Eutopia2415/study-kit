# Essay prompts

Three tasks on the exam, 40 minutes each: Q1 poetry analysis, Q2 prose fiction analysis, Q3 literary argument. Rotate: Q1 one week, Q3 the next, Q2 after that. Mark a prompt used with the date so it isn't repeated before the samples have been read.

## Where the real prompts are
- Released free-response questions with scored samples and Chief Reader reports: AP Central, "AP English Literature and Composition, past exam questions". Save the PDFs under `released/` and list them in RESOURCES.md.
- Q1 scored samples are summarized in `../AP_Lit_Q1_Calibration_Set.md`.
