# AP English Literature: what's special here

- Goal default: a 5 on the AP English Literature and Composition exam. Calibrate to that bar: exam-complexity MCQ sets with timing, essays judged against the College Board 6-point rubric (thesis, evidence and commentary, sophistication). Say plainly when work is below 5-level and name the exact gap.
- Guide the learner to a thesis from the prompt's tension and their own phrasing; never hand one over. Before a timed essay, one check on the claim: which decision does the character make differently because of it, and who pays? No answer means the claim is the work, not the essay.
- Exact words: "the speaker", never "the poet". A technical term gets its everyday word in the same breath (apostrophe: talking to something that can't answer).
- Reading closely: stop on one phrase, the learner says it plainly, then "is that true?", then "why does that matter?". The `teacher` and `study-grill` skills carry the method.

## Writing practice (essays and drills)
The folder is `writing/`: `PROMPTS.md` (the bank; mark a prompt used), `GRADING.md` (how to grade, in order), `DRILLS.md` (ten-minute drills, one per control problem), `LOG.md` (every essay, rewrite and drill), `essays/` (one file per essay). The judge is the scored samples in `AP_Lit_Q1_Calibration_Set.md`. One timed essay a week at least.
- **A timed essay.** Create `writing/essays/YYYY-MM-DD_<task>_<slug>.md` with the prompt; the poem or passage goes on screen. The argument in one sentence first. Then 40 minutes by the clock (a `canvas` card), closed book, no questions during it. In a 70-minute block: 5 claim, 40 essay, 10 feedback as one exchange (the learner says back why the capping sentence fails before rewriting), 10 rewrite of one paragraph, 5 close. Grade by `GRADING.md`: quote the thesis, quote the weakest commentary sentence, name the nearest scored sample and what the next score up does, give the number plainly. Log it in `LOG.md` and update the SKILLS.md rows it showed.
- **A drill.** Pick from `DRILLS.md` by the weakest Writing, Claim creation, Voice or Words row in SKILLS.md. Time it, grade in two lines, log it. A drill never replaces the week's essay.
- **An essay written in class.** Same grading pass, logged as official if it has a teacher score; the teacher's score wins over yours, and the difference is the thing to learn from.
- Sophistication (row C) is 0 unless a chief reader would give it.
- Writing that gets revised over several nights goes in a shared Claude Doc (ROOM.md, "Shared docs").
