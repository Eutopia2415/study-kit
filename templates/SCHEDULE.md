# Evening schedule

Study Hall's Schedule page holds the block times; change them there. Any subject chat can answer "what should I do tonight?" from this file and the subject STATE.md files.

## Rules
1. **Every subject often, short blocks.** 45 minutes each. A short block is still a block: two cold questions and one gap beat skipping the subject.
2. **Deadline first.** A test within 3 days takes its subject's block as a sweep of the whole test.
3. **Homework night:** cut the other blocks to cold questions only. Don't do three half-blocks of new material.
